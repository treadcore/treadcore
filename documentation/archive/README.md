# The archive

treadcore is a prototype that is improved a step at a time. When a
method or a document is replaced by a better one, the old one is not
deleted: it is archived here, as part of the development record. The
journal (`../../journal/`) and the changelog cite it, and a reader
should be able to follow any of those references back to what was
there.

| File | What it is | Archived |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ROADMAP-STEPS-0-11.md` | the roadmap as it ran from the opening day to step 11, every step's change, proof and finding word for word; replaced by `../ROADMAP.md`, set out by goal | 18 October 2026 |
| `BRAIN-METHODS.md` | the register of the bot AI's methods since step 12: which are archived (replaced as the default, most still one switch away), which are current with an improved challenger still off, and what was shown impossible | 18 October 2026, kept up to date |

Archived in place, marked where they stand, because their numbering is
cited:

- `../LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md` section 2: the brain as it stood on 17
  October, the baseline step 12 was measured against. Section 20 is the
  brain now.
- `../INDEX.md` sections 1 to 3 and 5: the code index as of commit
  `0874ff5` (13 October). Its section 6 adds what came since.

How something is archived: it gets a banner saying when and what
replaced it, a line in the table above (or in `BRAIN-METHODS.md` for a
brain method), and the documents that pointed at it point here.
