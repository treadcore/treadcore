## Abstract

A second project for the fleet's GPUs: a self-learning loop that runs
entirely on the card.
- **On the card:** the arena, the sensors, the population of nets, the
  play, selection and mutation, and a hall of fame of past champions.
- **Speed:** 581, 462 and 419 M tank-steps a second on the GTX 1080 Ti,
  1080 and 1070 Ti.
- **The decisive setting:** limiting registers to 128 doubled the
  speed.
- **Learning:**
  - self-play against the hall alone did not learn to beat a scripted
    yardstick (8.2 %);
  - with sparring bots it did on whitewolf (21.9 %, 3.4 standard
    errors);
  - its replicate on calber fell short of the bar (14.3 %, 1.8).

orrery's steps since CI-079 are also recorded here.

## III. Methods

1. **Build:** `make` on each GPU node (`nvcc -O3 -use_fast_math
   -gencode arch=compute_61,code=sm_61 -DHID=32 -DMINB=2`).
2. **Throughput:** `./crucible --pop 2048 --gens 4 --bench`.
   - whitewolf at MINB 1 to 4, and at H 48 and 64;
   - calber and boburner at the default.
3. **Learning:** `--pop 2048 --eps 128 --yard 10`, with `nvidia-smi`
   logged every 2 s:
   - whitewolf, seed 7, 300 generations, hall only;
   - whitewolf, seed 7, 400 generations, sparring 0.5;
   - calber, seed 8, 400 generations, sparring 0.5.
4. **The rule:** the last five yardstick reads (4,096 duels each) more
   than two standard errors above the hall-only run's last five.
5. **The records:**
   - crucible `docs/LAB-REPORT-GPU.md`, with every table;
   - the run files in crucible `runs/`.

**Errors.**
- `-DH` broke Thrust's headers; the macro is now `HID`.
- A zsh `$h:c` turned an rsync target into three local folders; they
  were removed.
- H = 64 does not launch (51.7 KB of shared memory against 48 KB). It
  is measured and kept as a result.

## V. Discussion

It can be done, and the cards are fast at it. They are not yet at
their maximum: 22 % of peak, and 55–66 % of their power. The report's
VII.B orders where the rest is:
- more duels per thread;
- fewer transcendentals;
- a warp sharing a net;
- INT8 `dp4a`.

The learning is real but slow. Sparring opponents mattered more than
the hall of fame alone. One replicate fell short, so the effect is
smaller than the first run says.

treadcore's own simulation is a poor fit as a whole, and a good one in
its combat core and its learned net.
