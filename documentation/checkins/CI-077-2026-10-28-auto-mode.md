## Abstract

The fleet's cycle now closes without a person:
- a search's result is adopted with its held-out reads;
- a candidate that passes both, with families that hold and current
  defaults, promotes itself;
- a promotion whose tests and families pass is pushed to main;
- every machine's build is then checked for the promoted values.

Candidates and other news are progress, not flags. The first full-size
spsa (r0014) and tune (r0015) runs cleared no bar on their own, and both
are adopted for held-out reads. The first auto promotion, r0019
(grove/w0034), is running.

## V. Discussion

The fleet already searched, held out and read families. What it lacked
was the last two steps and the proof between them. A promotion is now
measured against today's defaults, made by the fleet, and confirmed on
each machine by the values that machine compiled, not by the commit it
was told to build.

Auto mode trusts the bar that was already Paul's: two held-out reads
past two errors, every family over its floor, the brain's tests. Paul
can turn it off with one button.
