## Abstract

The learned tank now trains continuously beside the scripted brain:
`make school` runs its curriculum until stopped on the Mac, as `make
converge` runs the census on the census machine. A round keeps a net
only on a real gain with nothing broken, then gauntlets it. Two
training maps (low armour with full shells; an unguarded depot) answer
the shortcuts the gauntlet found. Each read first at 100% for the
scripted brain, 0% for the untrained net and 11% for the map 7 net.
More census machines would scale the run by whole censuses from a
shared queue; that is planned in CENSUS.md section 5a, to be built
when a machine arrives.
