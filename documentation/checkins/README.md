# Check-in reports

One report each time the work checks in with Paul, in the form of an
IEEE lab report. Each one gives the intent and Paul's prompts word for
word. It describes the methods in enough detail to repeat them, gives
the results with their errors, and logs every decision against the
prompt or the finding that caused it. Where the brain was touched, it
reviews the parts explored.

The journal (`journal/`) is the session's narrative and the lab reports
(`LAB-REPORT-*.md`) are the design and its measurements; a check-in
report is what ties a conversation to them. Named
`CI-NNN-YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`, numbered in order.
