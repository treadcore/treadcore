## Abstract

CI-017's planted test sat where memory barely acts. Run again with
perturbations wide enough to straddle the useful range (C 1.0), SPSA
moved `landfall_memory` from 5 s to 11.8 s in eight steps. Six steps
read the longer memory better, by +0.92 ± 0.62 a seat on average. The
tuner moves the right way once the slope is in view, but slowly: less
than halfway to 30 s in log terms after 96 seeds. The dodge's smaller
effects need a long run.

## V. Discussion

The planted test now says the tool works where it can see. It also
gives a scale: the largest effect measured moves the weight a factor
of 2.4 in 96 seeds. A tuner over effects a third that size needs about
ten times the seeds per unit of progress, which is what a long run
pays for.
