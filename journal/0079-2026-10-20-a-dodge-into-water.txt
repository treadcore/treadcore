               SCENARIOS OVER MANY SEEDS, AND A DODGE INTO WATER

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           20 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Scenarios now run over many seeds and report how often they hold.
    The circling scenario holds 100 of 100 with memory and 0 without,
    in under two seconds; any memory of 2 s or more breaks the circle.
    The shell scenario held 97 of 100: on the three, the dodge chose a
    point in the water, was refused, and sat under the shell. The fix
    takes the first reachable point. 1,000 of 1,000 then; in the
    census, the same 48 seeds before and after, 7.1% fewer deaths.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `run_seeded`, `many`, `set_weights`, `scenario_many`; brains
       given the map.
    2. Every scenario over 100 seeds; the memory's curve, a step at 2 s.
    3. The shell's three failures; `SEED=8` at a quarter second:
       "refused: water".
    4. `Dodge::order_on` with the map; 1,000 of 1,000.
    5. Before and after, two frozen binaries, the same 48 seeds. The
       first fix (water only afloat) made boats worse; a boat may now
       run onto a beach. 2,472 deaths, 2,390, then 2,297.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The dodge has been measured four ways since LR 27 (the death
    census, the hold, the switch off, SPSA), and each read it as a
    whole. A scenario run on a hundred grounds found the case where it
    did nothing at all.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

       scenario to test it.
