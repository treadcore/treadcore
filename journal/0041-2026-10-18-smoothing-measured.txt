ABSTRACT

    The rest of roadmap 12d is built, behind `smooth`. The Auto ladder
    confirms a change of rung over two thinks, except for a threat to
    the base. The decision to press or mass comes from the fight's odds
    averaged a think at a time, with a band either side (`advance_band`
    0.35, a new weight). This is Paul's "rounded off, averaged ... a few
    thoughts of confirmation" from 18 October. Measured with the fair
    census (journal 0040), six naval matches, the smoothed team scored
    7.55 a seat against 9.47 and asked as many Moves of the world. The
    bit stays off; its four weights go to the tuner.

I. METHOD

    `Commander::rung_challenger` and `Tactician::smooth`, with a test
    in `the_square_law`. Brain tests 55 passed, 8 ignored. The census:
    `CENSUS_CAPS=smooth`, naval.map, two seeds of 900 s, respawns 3,
    every team in the trial seat.

II. RESULTS

    Trial 7.55, baseline 9.47 a seat; a team's score across the six
    matches ran 3.28 to 14.29. Moves 4,661 a match.

III. DISCUSSION

    Holding a decision is not free: a rung whose work is done is left
    half a second late, and a fight is joined a think or two late. The
    first lesson of step 12 holds here too: a part goes in off, and
    the census says when it comes on.

IV. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. `make tune` over `confirm`, `beta`, `advance_band` and `margin`
       with `smooth` on.
    2. A dodge for direct fire.
