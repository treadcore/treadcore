                         INDUSTRY UNDER DRONES

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           13 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    A talk on the October strikes on Riyadh's airport and on Russian
    data centres made one point a game can use: infrastructure is the
    target now, the drones that hit it are cheap, and electronic
    warfare protects a point, never an economy. Roadmap step 10 was
    written from it and built the same night: pads and five structures
    (power plant, refinery, data centre, workshop, jammer), each
    serving its side while powered; the longshot, a slow one-way drone
    that crosses the map; concrete; a minimap and alerts with a key to
    jump to them; bots that take structures and send longshots; and a
    world map with the Pan-American Highway and Europe's trunk roads.
    The balance proofs on the generated island read the same to the
    digit. On the Atlantic map, sixteen bots in five minutes took 36
    structures and wrecked 20 with 43 longshots that landed, and the
    pillboxes at the chokepoints made 5 of the 12 kills, where on the
    island they make almost none.

INDEX TERMS

    structures, drones, electronic warfare, maps, user interface.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Paul's notes: drones of new kinds flying cross missions; buildable
    regions with more kinds of building (repair, data centre, refinery,
    power); tanks that protect the equipment and get its benefit; a
    commander's control of defence, a minimap, alerts of losing
    resources, territory battles; the maps finished, and a world map
    with a Pan-American Highway and a good Europe. And: it is a tank
    fighter, and stays one.

II. BACKGROUND

    Journal 0026 left PLAYABILITY item 11, the minimap, as next. The
    world had tanks, drones, pillboxes, depots and a squad that builds
    walls, roads and mines; nothing on the map was worth defending
    that was not one of those.

III. METHOD

    1. The plan first, in the roadmap's form: step 10, eight backlog
       items (10a to 10h), with the talk's five points as the reason
       for each. Written before any code.
    2. Must not change: the generated island. Every structure comes
       from a map's pads or `--industry`, so a bot-only match on the
       island has nothing new in it, and the brain's proofs are the
       check. A worktree at HEAD ran them for the numbers to compare.
    3. One rule each, a test each: power, each structure's benefit,
       capture and repair, the longshot's flight and its interception,
       the jammer on both sides, the data centre's scatter by mean of
       forty shells, concrete under two splashes.
    4. The window driven by xdotool at the shoot's 1200 x 740, as in
       0026, on the industrial island and on the Atlantic map.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

     1. ROADMAP.md step 10 written.
     2. map.rs: `Terrain::Pad` and `Terrain::Concrete`, glyphs `_`
        and `H`; `MapStructure` and `structure` lines;
        `Map::industrialise`.
     3. lib.rs and rules.rs: `StructureKind`, `Structure`,
        `World.structures`, `step_structures` (power, the stops, the
        refinery's saving given back after the drive, the workshop's
        mending, holding's score); `DroneKind::Longshot` and
        `DroneTask::Attack`, untethered so it flies on when its tank
        dies; the jammer at the head of `step_drones`; splash and
        direct damage to structures; the data centre in
        `FireIndirect` and in `observe`. Capture and Repair take a
        structure's id as well as a pillbox's, so the squad's orders
        did not grow. Seven tests; the first refinery test failed
        because the tank started inside the refinery's own stop and
        was refuelled as it drove.
     4. A worktree at HEAD, the brain's proofs run in both:
        defenders_hold, fortification_pays, pillboys_pay and the first
        minute read the same to the digit.
     5. Server: `--industry`, the economy line in the match report,
        the narration. The first report credited a longshot's damage
        to shells: the hit event came after the damage it did. It now
        comes first, as a strike's does.
     6. Bots: the RuleBot's squad takes structures after pillboxes, and
        a longshot goes every thirty seconds at the nearest hostile
        structure, a power plant first. The Defender takes and mends
        its own.
     7. The window: models for the five structures, concrete and the
        longshot; the works row (`0`, `B`); `X`; Shift for concrete;
        the LONG lamps; commander.rs with the alerts, the minimap and
        `J`. The first drive segfaulted: the musl stack, journal 0013's,
        and the drive script had not preloaded bigstack.so as
        shoot.sh does. With it, the frames showed the key hint cut off
        and the AIR panel squeezed to a sliver by the minimap. The
        drone lamps had reserved room for six lamps each; sized to
        their counts, the strip fits.
     8. scripts/worldmap.py. The first roads were straight lines
        between cities and crossed the sea in a dozen places; laid by
        a shortest path over the land they ran in staircases; with a
        pull toward the straight line they run as roads do. The first
        Europe had no Sicily, Sardinia or Crete: the Mediterranean was
        filled after them.

V. RESULTS

    make test passes: sim 33, brain 6, client 16, net 13, proto 3.
    The balance proofs are unchanged. Sixteen bots for five minutes:

                          industrial island   Atlantic   Europe
      structures taken             13            36          7
      longshots launched           27            78         65
      longshots landed             21            43         37
      structures wrecked            9            20          5
      kills (pillbox)           13 (0)        12 (5)      7 (2)

VI. DISCUSSION

    The world map did what the island never has: put pillboxes where
    the road has to pass. Five of twelve kills by pillbox, against one
    in seventy-four in 0010's proof, because the Darien Gap, Gibraltar
    and the Bosphorus are places a tank must drive through and the
    island has no such place. Backlog item 14 asked for this; a map
    answered it, not a rule.

    No drone was jammed in any of the three matches: no bot builds a
    jammer. The jammer is proven by test only, as the pillbox gun was
    before 6d.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Whether any of it is fun: the numbers in rules.rs are first
       guesses with reasons.
    2. No bot builds a structure or a jammer: every structure in
       those matches was on the map from the start.
    3. The bots' doctrine (25-tile standoff, a squad sent 20 tiles)
       was written for 128 tiles; on 512 they play their corner.
    4. Alerts are tested in a unit test, not yet seen in the window:
       no structure of the player's was hit while it was driven.
    5. The release frames (guide-*.png) predate the new rows and
       lamps.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. A playtest on the Atlantic map with the minimap and alerts.
    2. A bot that builds a jammer over its depot, and the trace of
       what that does to its own bubble.
    3. PLAYABILITY item 9, rookie bots.
