ABSTRACT

    its leader's depot. In a naval start the island is the beach's
    choice, so the beach now takes the site score off its cost,

    boat by a bare islet lands on the forested island in 400 of 400
    seeds, none at 0. The guard: +0.82 +/- 0.69 a seat over 48 seeds.
