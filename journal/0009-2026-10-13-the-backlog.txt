ABSTRACT

    The roadmap's backlog, fourteen items carried from the first traces
    (journal 0002), was worked through in one session: eight done, three
    decided from a trace with no change, three deferred to the step that
    owns them. The instrument came first: a match report at the end of
    every match and replay (item 8), then eight seeds of sixteen bots
    for 300 game seconds per variant. The one rule change that mattered:
    interceptors no longer engage interceptors. With it the kills a
    match go from 7.0 by shell and 5.9 by strike to 8.8 and 2.9, the gun
    is the weapon, and item 7 (the strike is the kill) answers itself.
    Tank speed 2.5 was tried and rejected: inside the noise, and the
    four-bot complaint was of a map that no longer exists. Added:
    Retask, forest concealment as the prior art had it, the three game
    types, the host's rtt in the HUD. 34 tests pass; wire version 9.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

     1. Read the backlog, the latest journal, the sim, the brain, the
        server. A baseline `make debug` of 300 s: 13 of 16 dead, 7 to
        shells, 6 to strikes, 32 interceptors shot down by
        interceptors, 0 pillbox shots, 8 refused orders (all one
        cause: a led indirect shot inside minimum range).
     2. The report: a `Report` struct in the server, fed every tick's
        events, printed after the scoreboard; a kill's cause is the
        last thing that hurt the tank, which a strike, a pillbox or a
        mine announces in the event before the damage, and anything
        else is a shell.
     3. A script that runs eight seeds and aggregates the six lines.
        Baseline, then variant A (the intruder filter in
        `step_drones` takes strikes only), then A + speed 2.5, then
        the four-player runs. Numbers in section V. A adopted; B not.
     4. Retask: the command, the event, the brain's use of it (a recon
        that cannot see the enemy is moved, not doubled). Seven
        retasks in the default trace.
     5. Concealment: `World::concealed` (a 2 x 2 block of forest round
        the tile), `tank_visible_to`, `spotted_by` and the pillbox
        target filter. The first test failed twice for reasons of its
        own: a recon recalled from inside recovery range is simply
        back in the bay; and the neutral pillbox the test placed six
        tiles east shot the recon parked two tiles from it. Forest
        also blocks a pillbox's shot by itself, so PIL-07 is belt and
        braces; the test says so.
     6. Game types: `Scenario.game_type`, `--game-type`, `GAMETYPE=`.
        Then the morning's replay would not parse (missing field);
        `#[serde(default)]` and it reads, and fails every hash from
        tick 100, which is the honest answer.
     7. Heartbeat `rtt_ticks`; version 9. The window crate built in
        release in 25 s against the engine `make warm` left, with its
        three old warnings and none new; it still cannot be run here.
     8. The brain's two refusal causes fixed; the report shows 0

     9. 34 tests; fresh trace and replay (36 of 36); lobby-test; docs;

VI. DISCUSSION

    The report paid for itself in the first hour: the baseline's "32
    interceptors shot down" was the backlog's item 1 as a number, and
    the variant's "6" (all pillbox air shots) was the decision. The
    real-world analogue says an interceptor engages anything; the
    game says the bubble is a shield. A shield that attacks the next
    shield leaves both tanks bare, and the trace showed what came
    through the gap.

    Speed was a non-result worth recording: the complaint in journal
    0002 was of four bots on a four-start map meeting in the middle
    in two minutes. On sixteen starts four bots take three minutes to
    find each other at either speed. The constant is a dial, and the
    hand on it should be a human's in a playtest.

    Concealment is in but unexercised: no brain stands in forest on
    purpose, so the trace shows nothing and the proof is a test. That
    is the right order; 6d's defender, which keeps forest standing
    for material, is where it will show.

    The old replay that would not parse is the lesson of the session:
    a scenario field added without a default turns every recording
    before it into a file that says nothing. A hash mismatch is a
    result; a parse error is a shrug.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    2. Pillboxes fire 0 to 2 times a match in bot play (item 14):

    3. Rejoin (item 10) waits for peer identity across processes
       (steps 7, 8).
    4. From 0008: no bot proposes or answers.
