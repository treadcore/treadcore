ABSTRACT

    `skill`, 1 in full, 0 a rookie, for every bot against the person.
    The duel: 63% at 1, 52% at 0.75, 24.5% at 0.6, 7.8% at 0.5.
    Nothing changed at 1.

    "bot skill dial. continue." Paul found the game hard.

II. BACKGROUND

    `--rookies N`, a flag a bot, read only for plain RuleBots.

III. METHOD

    A weight blending each rookie trait; the Commander spoiled at one
    place; the duel family swept.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. Found: in a teamed match, `make run`'s, `--rookies` reaches no
       bot. The guide's "start here" did nothing.
    2. `skill` in Settings; the RuleBot's traits blended or cut at 0.5.
    3. `Commander::blunt`: shells off, drones held.
    4. The sweep: a cliff at first look (63, 54.5, 7.8, 0). A direct
       shot's scatter cut to a tenth: the same. The doctrine's scatter
       doubled by the Commander's: made once; the same. Filled in, the
       curve falls steadily from 0.85 to 0.5.
    5. `--skill`, `SKILL=`; allies in full. The guide corrected.
    6. Killing the old demo, `pkill -f` matched the shell's own command
       line twice; the launch on its own.
    7. A match at 0.6 left open for Paul.

VI. DISCUSSION

    changed. A weight read where every bot is made does not.
