## Abstract

Every candidate of the morning was held by the trade guard: each
scored more by building less, since a thing built was worth a tenth of
a kill. Paul chose to count building and the base in the score, and its
numbers: a thing built 0.5; a repair 0.25; a load of wood felled within
16 tiles of a depot 0.2; the team's pillboxes' average life (3), the
longest one (2), turret kills (1) and their rate (2); the base's guns
ready (3). The score is versioned (SCORE_VERSION), and orrery reads
anew under it. On the same 48 seeds w8466 reads -0.42 +/- 0.15 a seat
under the new score against the old: the new score sees its trade.
