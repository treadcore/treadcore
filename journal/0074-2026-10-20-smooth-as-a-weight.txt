                           SMOOTH AS A WEIGHT

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           20 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The capability `smooth` becomes the weight `w_smooth`, 0.05 by
    default. Its two numeric parts blend; its four yes-or-no parts act
    above 0.5. Old files still load. The old play against the new
    default reads +0.47 +/- 0.60 over 48 seeds: insignificant, as Paul
    asked.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. The six places read. The stance's smoothing at a small share is
       a different rule, not a softer one, so it joined the yes-or-no
       parts; the retreat thresholds equal the constants today.
    2. The weight, the helpers, the old name kept as a reading of the
       weight in the parser and the census.
    3. A test caught a float: 1 - (1 - 0.4) is 0.39999998. The test
       was made to allow it.
    4. The null from a frozen binary, the docs written while it ran:
       the first run to use LR 45 that way.
