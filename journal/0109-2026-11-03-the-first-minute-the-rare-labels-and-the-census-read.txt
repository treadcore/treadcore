## Abstract

Paul's three lines from journal 0108. The census of the Quartermaster
was in by morning: −30.7 a seat over 400 seeds, and the lines he asked
for say the trained squad head works among sixteen (ore 7.8 a team
against the rules' 6.0, oil 5.1 against 0.0) and that nothing is smelted
of it, since a team of nets builds no smelter. The first-minute course
on the fleet's maps: the logistics net takes the pillbox already on two
maps and dies all the same, and forty generations did not move it. The
clone: the guard kept short put no more mines on the record (it was
short already; the smelter is the limit), so the recorder learned to
record the scripted brain on the courses themselves, where it mines six
times as often in a fourteenth of the samples; rare labels weighted up
to twenty make a better fighter of the clone and no miner; the matches
and the courses together make nets/bc4.bin, the first clone that mines
at all, two of ten on G17.

## Session

The census first, pooled from its 1,200 team lines against the default
teams in the same matches. Then the `pillboxes` count and the three
courses of ninety seconds, and a trainer run beside the rest. Then
RECORD_TRIAL, proved to reach the brains with a dial that changes the
deal, and byte for byte the same recording with the ore dials; the
course recorder, a Recorder brain round the Commander the file would
deal, the match recorder's window lifted into two functions both use;
RARE and RARE_HEADS in tools/bc, a rebuild, three clones and their
gauntlets.
