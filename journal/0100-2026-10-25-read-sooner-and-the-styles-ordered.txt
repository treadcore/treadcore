               READ SOONER, AND THE STYLES ORDERED

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           25 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    While the machine played the styles' round robin and team_rotate's
    re-read, the Mac built a sequential census: replayed over 44
    censuses (LR 78), then with an early stop read again on fresh
    seeds (LR 79). It holds false alarms to 2.8% but spends 106% of
    the seeds, so it stays an option. The round robin found no cycle:
    turtle first. Turtle and team_rotate off became the defaults,
    +1.75 +/- 0.19 a seat against the old. A family that fell at
    5899e8e was found and fixed in its test (81.5%). On the new
    defaults each part was read again: no default changes.

INDEX TERMS

    census, O'Brien-Fleming, round robin, defaults, scenario families.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Two long runs on the machine; the Mac's work in the meantime was
    backlog 59, then reading what the machine produced.

II. BACKGROUND

    Journal 0099's open problems; LR 75 (the front read off), LR 76
    (each default off again), backlog 60 (the round robin) queued.

III. METHOD

    The rule before each run, written to the run's NOTES.txt. Censuses
    on one machine each; the Mac only for scripts, families and tests.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. LR 78: 44 finished censuses replayed under group-sequential
       bounds; a quarter of the seeds saved, one false early stop.
       Committed (732fde3).
    2. LR 79: LOOKS=8 make census, an early stop read again on fresh
       seeds. Tested live on the Mac. Not the default (8062fb7).
    3. The round robin and team_rotate's re-read read: no cycle;
       team_rotate off +0.55 +/- 0.15.
    4. Turtle on 400 fresh seeds: +1.40 +/- 0.18. The pair against
       the old defaults: +1.75 +/- 0.19. Both in; classic.weights
       keeps the old (7c18851).
    5. make families: a-raid-on-a-guarded-base 70.2%; bisected to
       5899e8e. The family sets w_front_push 0.75 again: 81.5%.
    6. LR 81: each default off again on the new defaults, 200 fresh
       seeds. The dodge, posture and priced_crossing pay; team_rotate
       on costs atlantic; no default changes. Each scenario over 200
       seeds: ten at 100%, the two from the ready-gun family at 80%.

V. RESULTS

    LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md sections 76 to 81; CI-042 to CI-046.

VI. DISCUSSION

    Confirmation costs what an early stop saves, except on large
    effects. A fixture that tests a mechanism must hold it on when a
    default turns it off; make families at each default change
    catches the miss.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. A style built to beat turtle could close a ring (backlog 61).
    2. journal 0099's 1 to 3 carried.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Atlantic carries the board, the posture and team_rotate: a
       meter line there on the new defaults.
