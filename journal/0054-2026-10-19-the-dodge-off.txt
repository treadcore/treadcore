                             THE DODGE OFF

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           19 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The second causal test of the dodge. With it off for the trial
    team, 48 seeds read -2.45 a seat (error 0.67), outside the noise
    at 3.7 errors: the dodge pays, and the 43% of deaths it precedes
    are where the fire is. The meter, which still counts a dodge the
    switch drops, gives a rough counterfactual: ashore the dodge
    nearly halves the dodged shell's hits (about 30% to 16.6%);
    afloat it changes nothing. Backlog 28 closes; the dodge afloat is
    backlog 31.

I. INTRODUCTION

    Journal 0053 left this run going. It answers whether the reflex
    most deaths pass through saves tanks.

III. METHOD

    LR 29: `CENSUS_CAPS=-dodge`, 48 seeds in three ranges. The
    undodged rates estimated as three times the run's less twice the
    null's, on the assumption that each team draws a third of the
    shells.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. The run ended: -4.11, -1.39, -1.84 by range; -2.45 +/- 0.67.
    2. The meter still counted dodges near the null's number. Read
       `commander.rs`: `order_with` runs and meters, `dodges` drops
       the move after it. Not a fault in the reading: it makes the
       meter count would-be dodges, so the trial team's undodged
       shells are in the run's totals.
    3. Ashore: by the shell 21.1% mixed, about 30% undodged. Afloat:
       7.8% mixed, about 8% undodged, against 7.6% dodged.
    4. Backlog 31 written, after Paul's rule that an either/or is a
       weight: `dodge_afloat`.

VI. DISCUSSION

    Three readings of one reflex: the death census said it was where
    tanks died, the hold said its timing was not the fault, the
    switch said it saves 2.45 a seat. Only the switch is causal, and
    the death census alone would have pointed the wrong way. A
    switch-off run should come before reading any reflex's death
    share.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. The meter mixes teams and would-be with taken dodges.
    2. The dodge afloat (31); the landfall (30); the late parts (29).

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Backlog 31: the meter by team and by taken; then
       `dodge_afloat`.
    2. Backlog 30.
