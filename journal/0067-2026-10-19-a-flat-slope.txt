                 THE PLANTED TEST, FIRST RUN: A FLAT SLOPE

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           19 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    SPSA's planted test, `landfall_memory` from 5 s, ended at 3.69 s
    after eight steps whose differences were all inside their errors.
    Not passed. Most likely the plant sat where memory does nothing:
    the sightings that matter are 10 to 60 s old (LR 35), and the
    perturbations reached only 3 to 7.5 s. The test runs again with
    C 1.0, its two sides at about 14 s and 2 s.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The planted test was chosen for a weight with a known effect, but
    the effect was known between 0 and 30 s, not between 3 and 7.
    A planted value must sit on the slope as well as below the peak.
