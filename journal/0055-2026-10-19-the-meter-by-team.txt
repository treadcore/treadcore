                 THE METER BY TEAM, AND LR 29 CORRECTED

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           19 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Backlog 31 began with the meter, so the dodge could be read
    without an estimate. The meter now counts dodges by the seat's
    group (the census's trial team or the rest) and taken or dropped.
    Over 16 seeds of the dodge off, the same per-seed scores as LR 29:
    ashore the dodge halves the kills (15.0% to 6.6%); afloat it cuts
    them by a fifth (18.2% to 14.7%). LR 29's "nothing afloat" came
    from assuming equal shares of shells, and is withdrawn.

III. METHOD

    `meter::trial`, `meter::seat(p)`, `Dodge::dropped`, the nested
    `Dodges` counts; a test of the buckets; `-dodge` over seeds 0 to
    15 in three ranges side by side.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. The meter split; one test; brain 70 passed, 8 ignored.
    2. The run: per-seed differences equal to LR 29's first range.
    3. The rates: afloat, dropped 18.2% killed against 14.7% taken.
       The trial team without the dodge drew 1,899 ashore dodges
       against about 2,500 a team: LR 29's equal-shares assumption
       was false.
    4. LR 29 marked; backlog 31 weakened and moved after 30.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The estimate in 0054 was labelled a direction, and the direction
    was wrong for boats. An instrument that needs an assumption to be
    read should be fixed before its reading drives a backlog item;
    here it was fixed one step later, before any code followed from
    it.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Backlog 30: the run for the beach under direct fire.
    2. Backlog 31 after it, as a trade for the tuner.
