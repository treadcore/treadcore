                    THE WORKS TRIP, AND WHO HEARS IT

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           14 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Two items from 0029's next steps. A RuleBot with the scrap for what
    a structure of its own wants now drives to it and holds while the
    squad builds: on the Atlantic map twelve structures in five minutes
    where 0029 built six. The industrial island still builds none, and
    the session found out why by probing rather than by tuning. And
    chat reaches all four of the prior art's recipients: everyone, the
    team, the tanks nearby, and players chosen with @name.

INDEX TERMS

    bots, user interface, debugging.


I. INTRODUCTION

    0029 left "a bot that goes to its zone to build" and "PLAYABILITY
    item 6's rest" as next. Both done; the balance proofs read the same
    to the digit.

II. BACKGROUND

    obs.pads lists the free pads within twenty tiles of the tank, so a
    bot cannot see a far zone's room; structures are public.

III. METHOD

    The works were split: works_need says what the bot's structures
    want within a reach, without pads; works_order picks the pad when
    in reach. The trip drives at the structure, and the pad is found on
    arrival; a structure with no room for a kind is remembered, so the
    bot does not shuttle between its zone and its firing position.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. works_need, works_order, no_room, the trip at WORKS_TRIP (45
       tiles). Atlantic 9 built; the island 0.
    2. Probe 1 (temporary eprintln, removed): an enemy was in view in
       16 of 237 samples on the island; bots stood 5 to 11 tiles from a
       structure that wanted something, with 8 scrap. The guess that
       the island was too crowded to be quiet was wrong.
    3. Probe 2: the squad section ran only away from the zones. A bot
       arriving in reach was sent on to its firing position the next
       think, before the settled second the squad waits for. Fix: hold
       in reach while the scrap is there. Atlantic 12 built; the
       island 0.
    4. Probe 3: works_order ran once on the island all match and chose
       a good pad; the build was ordered. A structure takes twenty
       seconds; on 128 tiles the bot is hit before that and the squad
       comes home, by design. The island is too hot to build on: a
       reading, not a bug.
    5. a_rulebot_drives_to_its_zone_to_build: a dark refinery thirty
       tiles out, beyond the squad's reach; the bot drives there and
       powers it.
    6. Chat: ChatTo::Nearby (CHAT_NEARBY, 15 tiles of the sender's
       tank, decided by the world's event_visible) and
       ChatTo::Players(mask), one bit a seat; a list with only the
       sender refused ("nobody chosen to say it to", KB-TO-CHAIR, which
       every_refusal_has_a_name demanded). The window: Tab steps all,
       team, nearby; leading @words choose (a name, a unique start, or
       p and a seat; an exact name wins over starts); a word that is
       nobody keeps the line open with a LOOSE NUT note. The prompt
       says who and how many; nearby counts the tanks seen, and says
       so.

V. RESULTS

    make test: sim 36, brain 10, client 19, net 13, proto 3. Balance:
    16:4, 16950:10720, 35:34, 198; rookies 9:1; defenders 105 of 120.

VI. DISCUSSION

    Three probes found what three guesses would not have. The second
    was the real bug (a hold that was never there); the third was the
    game working.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    3. Chat's recipients are seen only in tests and the prompt; not yet
       in a frame.
    4. The guide's frames predate the chat prompt's audience.
