                       WHAT KILLS HALF THE TEAM

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           19 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Backlog 28's look. The session finished instruments left in the
    tree: a death census by status code and by weapon, a meter on
    the dodge, a code for the run for the beach, and the dodge's
    constants as weights with one new one, `dodge_hold`. Over 16
    seeds, 819 deaths: the dodge is the code held last at 43% and
    `landfall` at 20%, in 7.7% of the time. Afloat, one dodge in six
    ends in a kill. The dodge precedes deaths because it answers fire,
    so the causal test, the dodge off and `dodge_hold` 1 at 48 seeds,
    is the next step.

INDEX TERMS

    attrition, instrumentation, reflexes, survival, census.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Journal 0051 found half the team's seat-time dead and named
    backlog 28: what was a seat doing when it went down.

II. BACKGROUND

    The tree held uncommitted work from the end of 0051: the death
    tables in `brain_census`, `Dodge::watch` and its meter,
    `Brain::dead`, `Act::Landfall`, four dodge weights. Not yet built
    or measured. Environment as before.

III. METHOD

    Build and test what was in the tree; regenerate the brain map;
    one 16-seed null census with `CENSUS_ACTS=1`; then the causal
    pair at 48 seeds (running at commit). The brain overview (LR 20)
    brought up to date.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `cargo test -p treadcore-brain --release`: 69 passed, 8
       ignored, including the new dodge-hold test.
    2. `make brainmap`: 124 undocumented; the two new meter functions
       given doc lines, 122.
    3. The 16-seed null census, 372 s: trial 8.38, baseline 8.38, 0 on
       every seed. 819 deaths.
    4. The tables read: dodge 43.2% of deaths last held, 67.3% in the
       5 s before; landfall 20.1%; direct fire on a boat at landfall
       the commonest single pair, 132.
    5. The causal runs launched: `dodge_hold` 1 (a weights file), then
       `-dodge`, 48 seeds each in three ranges of 16.
    6. Paul: "commit. push. continue backlog". The look committed on
       its own; the causal runs are the next entry's.

V. RESULTS

    LR 27. Dodges: ashore 7,911, a third hit, a sixth by the shell
    dodged, 7.4% killed; afloat 3,408, 18% hit, 16.6% killed. Tests:
    brain 69 passed and 8 ignored; client 31; net 14; proto 3; sim 50.

VI. DISCUSSION

    A reflex is where a tank is when it is in danger, so a death
    census by code overstates it. The share of time against the share
    of deaths is the lead (9.6 times for the dodge, 6.3 for landfall),
    and only the switch off can say whether the reflex helps.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Does the dodge save tanks or lose them (backlog 28)?
    2. The run for the beach under direct fire (backlog 30).
    3. A census where late parts act (backlog 29).

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Read the `dodge_hold` 1 and `-dodge` censuses against the null.
    2. Backlog 30, the landfall.
