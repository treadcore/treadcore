## Abstract

Paul chose the second of orrery's three GPU steps: "yes do step 2, the
duelist with world senses." crucible's islands have trained under the
game's rules since the evening before (55 inputs, 48, 48, 4 out), and
the bridge of LR 126 read only the toy arena's hybrid. The bridge now
reads a world champion too: its twenty world inputs built from what a
tank sees as crucible's world sense builds them, its eyes ending at ten
tiles, a heading-error turn and no reverse. A new slot, 17, the
Gladiator, ships the world milestone M0015, so style 7 keeps the hybrid
and one binary reads both. On the same 48 seeds the Gladiator reads
+4.99 +/- 2.08 a seat over the Duelist on every map, and +11.56 +/- 1.75 on the
generated maps. GLADIATOR=file lets the fleet read a champion not yet
shipped; orrery's bridge reads each new world milestone against the
shipped one, and shipping is a press.
