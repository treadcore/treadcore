          THE DUCK: DIRECT FIRE DENIED BEFORE IT IS FIRED

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Journal 0045 found that a direct shot cannot be dodged once fired;
    Paul chose the duck. Behind the bit `duck`, a tank leaves a hostile
    gun's range or line when that gun will be ready before its own,
    with its armour share at or under `w_duck`. It took four duels to
    prove, and the fourth found the real problem. Sight and direct
    range are both 10 tiles, so the gun that fires is often unseen.
    Guns are now remembered for `duck_memory`, refreshed by any direct
    shell of theirs in flight. Duel: hit 2 times ducking against 3
    standing. Fair census: 7.01 a seat against 7.52, inside the noise;
    the bit stays off.

I. METHOD

    Commander::duck and its place after the dodge; weights `w_duck` 1,
    `duck_ready` 1.5 s, `duck_memory` 5 s; the bit `duck` (19). Proof:
    `a_tank_ducks_a_gun_ready_before_its_own`, the rules and a 40 s
    duel against a standing gunner firing a led shot whenever it can.
    Census: `CENSUS_CAPS=duck`, naval.map, two seeds of 900 s, respawns
    3, and a "ducking" count.

II. RESULTS

    The duels, in order:
      1. 3 hits each: a tank is down after three, so the count capped.
      2. 3 each, my gun silenced: a ready gun does not duck, by the
         rule, and the duel had silenced it.
      3. 1 each: return fire and strike drones ended the match by 3.5 s.
      4. 3 each, my gun held reloading and only its moves passed: at
         the second shot the gun, 10.3 tiles off, was not in my
         observation.
    With the memory, a 13-tile margin and the shells' `from`: 2
    against 3. Brain tests 62 passed, 8 ignored; client 31. Census
    7.01 against 7.52; ducking in all six matches, 2 to 16
    seat-seconds in five and 567 in one.

III. DISCUSSION

    Each failed duel was the test telling the truth about the test
    first, and the last one about the game: a tank cannot see the gun
    that is about to hit it. The duck is right in a duel. In a match a
    tank on an errand may need to be in reach to do its errand, which
    is the trade `w_duck` weighs. Whether ducking wins matches is for
    longer censuses and the tuner, like the rest of step 12.
