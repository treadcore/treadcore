                  EACH SWITCH ALONE; THE POSTURE READ

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The roadmap's next measurement was every switch of the step-12
    brain alone at twelve seeds. The census now gives its noise as a
    number, the standard error of the paired difference over seeds.
    Eleven switches were measured, 396 matches in 22 minutes: none was
    outside two errors, and three never acted. The team board, in
    particular, changed no decision. The board's posture was made to
    lean the Auto ladder through a blend. Its first match found the
    posture broken (Defend 94% of the time), and that was fixed. Twelve
    seeds then read +1.21 +/- 1.31 a seat, and 48 read -0.81 +/- 0.65.
    It stays off. Lesson: a part never read is never tested, and a
    reading inside its error is no reading.

INDEX TERMS

    paired census, standard error, power, influence map, posture,
    utility blend, evolution strategy, SPSA.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Sixteen capabilities were built and off, none promoted, and every
    "inside the noise" so far was judged by eye from two or three seeds.
    The session set out to measure each alone, fairly and with a stated
    error, and to act on what that showed.

II. BACKGROUND

    Open from 0048: the Observation shared per team (12b); backlog 18,
    twelve seeds a switch. The environment as in 0048: Alpine guest,
    rustc 1.98.1, release builds. Actions paused; no tags.

III. METHOD

    The fair census (LR 14) on naval.map, 900 s, respawns 3; the paired
    difference a seed and its standard error over seeds, "outside" past
    two errors (LR 24, CI-001 III). Three censuses side by side on the
    four cores, the ledger off so their lines do not interleave. A
    regression test is run on the old code and must fail there (0048's
    practice).

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `ledger::mean_se` and the census's per-seed line. A null run read
       0 +/- 0 on both seeds, as it must.
    2. `scripts/switches.sh`, `make switches`. The verdict column split
       on spaces; fixed.
    3. Eleven switches, twelve seeds each. `board` read 0.00 on every
       seed, its matches identical to the baseline's number for number.
       Traced: no decision outside board.rs reads influence, the front
       or the posture. `island_site` read 0.00: a fleet tank always has
       its leader's depot first. `escort` +0.01: one far fetch in 36
       matches.
    4. The posture made to lean the ladder: `Rung::pick`, `w_posture`,
       `posture`, a meter counter for the picks.
    5. The first match: 3.64 against 11.02. The meter: Defend 94% of
       picks. The threat field's long tail: eight tanks 80 tiles off add
       0.73 at the base. Also the readiness spread over 0.6. Fixed:
       Defend within the base's alarm; the spread left to rotation. The
       new test fails on the old rule.
    6. At w 0.25 nothing changed (the leanings sit two rungs below); at
       0.5 and 1 the matches are identical, as the formula says. Twelve
       seeds: 0.35 +0.39 +/- 0.36; 0.5 +1.21 +/- 1.31.
    7. `CENSUS_SEED0`; seeds 12 to 47 in three ranges side by side:
       -1.30, -2.16, -1.01. All 48: -0.81 +/- 0.65. The first twelve
       were the dice. Default 0.5 kept for the tuner; the switch off.
    8. Paul at the check-in asked for an IEEE-form report each check-in:
       the prompts, how they caused decisions, the methods in detail,
       a review of the brain's architecture, the tuner and mutation,
       faster playtests, and the backlog's form. CI-001 is that report;
       `make brainmap` writes the function index from the code.
    9. `make test`: green.

V. RESULTS

    Each switch alone, difference a seat (error): smooth -1.98 (1.25),
    team_rotate -0.83 (0.87), forget -0.36 (0.23), column -0.06 (0.41),
    board 0 (0), island_site 0 (0), escort +0.01 (0.01),
    priced_crossing +0.11 (0.56), cycle +0.31 (0.43), duck +0.39
    (1.04), bridge_jobs +0.44 (0.91). Posture at 0.5, 48 seeds: -0.81
    (0.65). Throughput: about 1,080 matches an hour on three cores, a
    900 s match in 10 to 11 s on one (85x). Tests: brain 67 passed and
    8 ignored; client 31; net 14; proto 3; sim 50. New files:
    scripts/switches.sh, scripts/brainmap.py, documentation/BRAIN-MAP.md,
    documentation/checkins/.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The identical matches were the finding the scores could not give:
    a part that never acts reads "even", and even is not good. The
    board drawn and unread had a bug nobody could see until something
    read it. The posture's twelve-seed reading looked like a result and
    was not. Confirmation on fresh seeds is part of measuring. The same
    arithmetic says the tuner, at two seeds, selects noise (CI-001 VI).
