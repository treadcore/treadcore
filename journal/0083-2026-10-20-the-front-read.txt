    backlog's items, and keep proving each by tests, faster where it
    can be. CI-025 had left 49 (the front read) and 50 (allies' goals)
    as the next features, 47 (floors) half done, 26 open.

II. BACKGROUND

    Every part on by default (LR 55); new parts land on with starting
    numbers and the census guards (LR 54's rule). 96 weights, 17 bits;
    nine scenario files, five families; brain 78 tests.

III. METHOD

    One item at a time; a scenario that fails with the part at 0
    before the census; the census from a frozen binary in three jobs
    on four cores, other work going on beside it. A census of a part
    the meter says hardly acts was stopped rather than finished.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `w_front` in `Commander::expand`; scenario keys `board`,
       `rungs`, `[[depot]] at`, `depot_first`. The first scenario put a
       depot off the map's edge; the second had the hostile out of
       sight, so no front: a teammate placed near it to see for the
       team.
    2. The trace: the tank went for the front depot, then turned back
       at 4% of the capture; its own arrival outweighed the hostile and
       the front vanished. The pick is kept until taken.
    3. The guard census begun; at 60 matches the meter read 2.5 picks a
       match with a front known. Stopped.
    4. `front_memory`: the board keeps hostile tanks where last seen,
       for the front alone. `a_front_seen_is_remembered`. Census again:
       3.2 picks a match with a front, 14% without.
    5. Beside it: `make families` and floors (47); the screen test
       (50); the crew and hop tests and doc comments (26).
    6. The guard: -0.14 +/- 0.42 a seat over 48 seeds.

V. RESULTS

    LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md sections 58 to 60. Tests: brain 81 passed, 11
    ignored; client 32; net 14; proto 3; sim 50. 98 weights, 17 bits;
    ten scenario files, five families with floors.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The board's sight is the bots' sight, and a front of what is in
    sight comes and goes each second. The landfall learned this in LR
    35 and the front learned it again: a thing read from the board
    wants a memory under it. And a part that touches a few decisions a
    match (Expand picks about four depots) will read inside the noise
    whatever it does; the meter's count is what says it acts.
