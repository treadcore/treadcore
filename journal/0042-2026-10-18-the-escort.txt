                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The last behaviour of Paul's base cycle, the mission. A fleet
    leader whose fetch trip runs past `fetch_gather` (20 tiles) now
    gathers its wingmen at the base before it goes, behind the bit
    `escort`. The fleet then goes out in its wedge and screens the carry
    home, and the router brings the pillbox along the road, which costs
    0.6 a tile against grass's 1.0. In a match with one wingman 45
    tiles off, the leader gathered and its squad lifted the pillbox by
    324 s. The fair census of six naval matches read exactly even:
    nobody reached a far fetch in 900 s, so the bit never acted. It
    stays off until longer matches can show it.

I. METHOD

    `Commander::turrets` gathers before a far fetch when `escort` is on
    and the leader has members. Proof: `a_far_fetch_gathers_the_fleet`.
    Census: `CENSUS_CAPS=escort`, naval.map, two seeds of 900 s.

II. RESULTS

    Brain tests 56 passed, 8 ignored. Census 7.70 against 7.70.

III. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. A census long enough to reach the turrets on naval.map, which
       means goal 5's longer matches.
    2. Bridge jobs on the team board.
