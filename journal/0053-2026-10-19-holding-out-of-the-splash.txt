                       HOLDING OUT OF THE SPLASH

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           19 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The first causal test of the dodge. If tanks were caught after
    reaching their dodge point, holding there until the shell lands
    (`dodge_hold` 1) would save them. Over 48 seeds it costs 1.18 a
    seat (error 0.55, just outside two) and leaves the dodged shell's
    hits where they were, 16.7% of ashore dodges against 16.6%. The
    hits come during the drive. `dodge_hold` stays 0.

I. INTRODUCTION

    Journal 0052 found the dodge last held at 43% of deaths and named
    two causal runs. This entry is the first; the second, the dodge
    off, is still running.

III. METHOD

    LR 28: a defaults dump with `dodge_hold = 1` as the trial team's
    weights, 48 seeds in three ranges side by side, the null as
    reference.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. The hold run ended: -2.02, -0.38 and -1.14 by range; combined,
       -1.18 +/- 0.55 over 48 seeds.
    2. The meter, all Commanders: ashore by the shell 16.7%, as the
       null; afloat killed 13.9% against 16.6%, diluted by the
       baseline teams.
    3. Paul: "commit. push. continue backlog". Recorded as its own
       step while the dodge-off run goes on.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The hypothesis named where in the dodge the hits fall and was
    wrong about it; the meter's by-the-shell count was the test, and
    it did not move. A weight that holds the tank still has a cost
    the meter does not show and the score does.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. `-dodge` at 48 seeds against the null.
    2. `dodge_horizon` and `dodge_clear` to the tuner (backlog 23).
    3. Backlog 30, the landfall.
