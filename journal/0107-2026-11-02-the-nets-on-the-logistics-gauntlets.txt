## Abstract

Paul: "train the nets on the gauntlets." The learned tank could not: its
squad head was scored and never acted on, so the zeros of games 6 to 8
on G13, G14 and G17 were the mask's. The head is wired (capture, build a
smelter, mend, fell, mine or pump, home, each offered within eight
tiles), the three courses pay the drive to the works as a potential, and
the trainer took the five files as a course. From the clone the search
was flat; from the evolution milestone it learned the drive and the raid
and never the mine, because an untrained head carried through a thousand
generations scores its choices two hundred apart. A head reset to zero
at the start of training is the fix: from map7 with the squad head at
zero, every course passed held out in 300 generations, G13 19 of 20, G14
20, G15 20, G16 17, G17 20. The net is nets/logistics.bin.

## Session

The squad head first, with its test and the recorder's label. A probe of
two generations showed the trainer at four seconds a generation and the
nets mining where they stood without driving, so the courses gained the
`closer` potential toward the works. Three runs of 300: from bc2, every
perturbation 3.000 from generation 6 (a clone that stands still in an
empty field has no gradient); from map7, the raid learned to 20 of 20
and the drive west, no mine; from the 300th net at a wider step, the
same. The probe `squad_head_margin` gave the cause in numbers: bc2
scores mine over hold by four, map7 scores hold over mine by 229. The
reset (`Net::zero_head`, `RESET=squad`) and the third run from map7:
passed 1.00 by generation 20, every course held out.
