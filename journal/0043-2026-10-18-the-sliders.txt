         THE SLIDERS: THE COORDINATION SCREEN'S WEIGHTS PAGE

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    "A slider for each coefficient weights", from the first message of
    step 12. `U` on the coordination screen opens the weights page: a
    switch for each of the 15 capabilities and a slider for each of the
    70 weights, its default at the middle of a bar and its doc line
    under the rows. It is the player's own team's: the tank on auto
    and, when the window hosts, the bots of the player's side, at once
    and at each new match. Enter saves to ~/.config/treadcore/weights.
    `--weights FILE` stays every bot's. Driven in the window: a switch
    and a slider moved, put back and closed.

I. METHOD

    Brain: `W::step` (one for a rounded weight, else a tenth of the
    default to 1, 2 or 5 of its power of ten), `Settings::nudge`,
    `Caps::doc`, `Brain::set_settings`. `Commander::set_settings` now
    updates the tactician in place and gives back a switch's setting
    when it is turned on again (`CapHeld`). Client: `tune.rs`, the
    `tune` action on U, `View::tune` among the keys taken.

    The window: build/bigstack.so preloaded (journal 0013; without it
    lavapipe segfaults on musl's small thread stacks), a solo match,
    keys sent to the window with `hyprctl dispatch sendshortcut` (not
    to whatever had focus), shots with grim.

II. RESULTS

    Brain tests 58 passed, 8 ignored; client 31. In the window: Space
    then U showed the page; Down to `near_scale`, Right twice took it
    from 15 to 19 with the `*`; Delete put it back; Esc returned to the
    ladder with the rotation and the fleet. Left on `forget`, already
    off, left it off. Enter was not pressed: it would have written the
    real team file.

III. DISCUSSION

    The page changes who can tune, not how well the bots play: a
    player can try a weight in the match in front of them, and the
    saved file is a genome the ledger reads. Two things the page found
    out about the brain: the window's pilot never had settings at all
    (it now has the page's), and four switches could only be turned
    off. Switches read only at the deal (`board`) take hold at the next
    match.

IV. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. The page's settings into the ledger when a match ends, so a
       player's tuning is scored like the tuner's.
    2. A remote player's sliders reach only their own tank; the host
       could take a team's settings over the wire.
    3. Bridge jobs on the team board (0042's second).
