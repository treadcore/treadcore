                  THE DODGE PLANNED BY THE TANK'S OWN MOTION

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           20 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    LR 52 left a tenth of ashore dodges caught by their own shell. A
    scenario family, one file whose geometry is drawn from the seed,
    put today's dodge at 89.6% for a rolling tank and 96.2% standing
    over 1,000 seeds each. Traced, the failures were two: a point
    behind a rolling tank, two legs of the world's route away, and a
    tank that arrived early sent back under the shell by its errand.
    `drive_to` foresees the world exactly; `dodge_plan` and
    `dodge_release`, both off, take both families to 100%. The census:
    +0.92 +/- 0.47 (naval), +1.20 +/- 0.60 (generated), +0.70 +/- 0.66
    on fresh seeds, not confirmed alone; own-shell catches ashore cut
    by 40% in every run.

INDEX TERMS

    dodge, motion model, route, scenario family, fresh-seed rule.


I. INTRODUCTION

    The open lead of LR 52: ashore, 10.4% of dodges caught by their own
    shell. The dodge reads the shell's line and not the tank's motion,
    though the impact is exact once fired.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `[[tank]]` speed and heading; `[[shell]]` offset, scatter,
       from_any, a lands_in range; the trace shows routes and shells.
    2. A fixed case (rolling east, a shell 1.5 tiles ahead in 2 s) on
       `manual` held: a Commander on manual runs no brain. On auto it
       turned back and arrived at 1.9 s of 2.0. One case says little.
    3. The families, 1,000 seeds: 89.6% rolling, 96.2% standing.
    4. Traces: F1 the turnaround, F2 the release.
    5. `dodge_plan` driving a straight line: 89.7%. The world routes by
       tiles in eight directions; the plan foresaw the wrong drive.
    6. `drive_to` with `route_by` and corner braking: 94.5%. Its test
       read 0.000 at first because the check of "it drove" was wrong;
       mended, then broken two ways, it fails by 1.7 and 4.7 tiles.
    7. `dodge_release`: 95.0% and 99.2% alone; with the plan 99.9% and
       100%. The last failure: straight on ran 14 tiles into a neutral
       pillbox. Straight on now stops at the 5-tile circle: 100%, 100%.
    8. `seed = n` in a file; two families' cases kept as tests, each
       failing with its weight at 0. A first form of the pin worked in
       `cargo test` but not in `make scenario`; moved into run_seeded.
    9. The census first ran without CENSUS_MAP and CENSUS_RESPAWNS, on a
       generated map: +1.20 +/- 0.60, kept as a second map. Then
       naval.map, 3 respawns: +0.92 +/- 0.47. The meter's plain count
       is the rest of the seats, not all of them; a first pooling read
       it wrong.
   10. Fresh seeds 240 to 287: +0.70 +/- 0.66. Not confirmed; the
       mechanism repeated to the tenth.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The census needed three runs and an hour to bring the score near
    two errors; the families settled the mechanism in three seconds
    each and pointed at both causes. Four of the five false starts in
    this entry were checks that could not fail, or read the wrong
    thing: the manual goal, the "it drove" assertion, the pin that
    `make scenario` skipped, the meter's groups. Each was caught by
    running the check broken, or by a number that did not fit.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Paul: `dodge_plan` and `dodge_release` on by default, or not.
    2. Backlog 45 (two shells), 46 (the duck by `drive_to`), 47
       (families kept with floors), 42 (`duck`).
