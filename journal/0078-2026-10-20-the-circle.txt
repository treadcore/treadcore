                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org

ABSTRACT

    Backlog 43's premise, from LR 49, was wrong: a trace every 5 s
    missed a boat that landed in under 5. A trace every second found
    two real things: `Commander::new` did not apply its own default
    Settings (fixed), and without memory a boat circles at the edge of
    sight, ashore at 40 s beside the gun rather than at 6 s on the safe
    coast. That is the mechanism of the memory's gain.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `trace_every` and positions in the trace.
    2. The Commander at sea: `landfall` from 1.9 s; LR 49's finding 1
       withdrawn.
    3. Two runs identical again: neither called `set_settings`.
       `Commander::new` fixed; tests pass.
    4. The circle seen; the scenario written; it fails at memory 0.
    5. A census re-run of LR 46's first range started, to check dealt
       play unchanged; committed before it ended, on Paul's prompt.

VI. DISCUSSION

    A trace sampled slower than the event it watches will report the
    state after the event, every time. The 5 s default hid a 5 s
    crossing.
