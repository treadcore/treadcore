## Abstract

Paul asked for a coded set of tests that is the boundary of smart play:
attack some pillboxes and avoid others, answer the enemy that last
attacked, kite it alone, stand and fight with backup. Five gauntlets,
G18 to G22, drawn on the game's own rules (a gun of ten against a ring
of eight; a live hostile pillbox shot dead before it is taken, a live
neutral one taken whole). Three expectations new to the scenario form.
On 50 seeds no brain meets them; the rules read 24, 0, 48, 34 and 56%,
each failing as its file says. The 400-seed read of the Gladiator's gate
at 0.6 (+0.08 +/- 0.40) left its default at 0.

## Session

Code: scenario.rs (pillbox_struck, first_hit, pillboxes_standing).
Files: scenarios/gauntlet/g18 to g22. Reads: the gauntlet for the rules,
the untrained net, the milestones, bc2 and logistics, 50 seeds; each
file played by the rules on 20 seeds for its failures in words; G22
corrected twice (friends that escorted, two hits too many). LR 141,
CI-090, CHANGELOG, ROADMAP 83, this entry, the brain map. In orrery the
trade guard's floors for building and road became shares (10%).
