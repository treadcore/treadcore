## Abstract

Paul asked for a sensor that "will indicate a ring, as it would indicate
and identify the foe". Six numbers were added to the senses, last, so
every net widens with zeros: the whole gauntlet read the same to the seed
for every shipped net. Five nets imitating the rules with the ring and
five without, trained on the same 163,510 samples, read alike on G18 and
G19 and worse with it on G7. The rule was not met; the block is parked
off main. G18 needed experience, not eyes; G19 needs a steadier training.
