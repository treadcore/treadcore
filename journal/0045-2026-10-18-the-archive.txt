          THE ARCHIVE: THE OLD METHODS KEPT AS THE RECORD

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Two things. First, a measurement: the dodge for direct fire, the
    next roadmap step, cannot be built as asked. A led direct shot
    hits whatever the target does once it is fired, from 4, 7 and 9.5
    tiles, and a kept test says so. Second, the documentation now
    separates the improved prototype from what it replaced. A new
    folder, documentation/archive/, holds the old roadmap and a
    register of the brain's methods: nine archived, ten current with
    an improved challenger off, one shown impossible. The lab report's
    baseline section and the index's old snapshot are marked archived
    where they stand, since their numbers are cited, and each gained a
    section for what is current.

I. METHOD

    The direct-fire question: a throwaway experiment first, made the
    test `a_led_direct_shot_cannot_be_dodged` once it held (lib.rs).
    The documents: every claim of the register checked against the
    code, the tests and the censuses of journals 0038 to 0044; line
    counts and test counts taken the same evening.

II. RESULTS

    Direct fire: hit at every range and every answer. Brain tests 61
    passed, 8 ignored; client 31. Archive: archive/README.md,
    archive/BRAIN-METHODS.md, archive/ROADMAP-STEPS-0-11.md (moved,
    with a banner). LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md: a guide to reading it, section
    2 marked archived, sections 19 (direct fire) and 20 (the brain
    now). ROADMAP.md: goal 4 "the prototype built", 12d's finding,
    backlog 3 and 8 annotated, 17 to 19 new. INDEX.md: the snapshot
    marked, section 6 for step 12's files, the documents table
    complete. README: the brain report, the proposal, the archive.
    Code: two comments mark the old castle and the old census as
    archived.

III. DISCUSSION

    Nothing step 12 replaced was thrown away: the old castle, the old
    census and the brain of 17 October all still run, one switch away,
    and the census needs them as the baseline. Archiving is a label
    and a pointer, not a deletion. The honest line in the lab report's
    section 20 is the last one: the prototype is better built, but no
    new part has yet been shown to win more matches.

IV. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Backlog 17, Paul's choice: a duck reflex, close the item, or a
       rules change.
    2. Backlog 18: twelve seeds for each switch alone, and longer
       matches for the late-paying parts.
    3. Backlog 19: the 70 partial_cmp().unwrap() calls.
