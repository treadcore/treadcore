                     THE POST-MORTEM AND THE PLAN

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           2 November 2026


ABSTRACT

    A week of training without a hand on it ended, and the session read
    it back: twenty-eight reports and design documents a section at a
    time, the roadmap, the brain report, the check-ins, the journal, the
    code and the week's data, into a book of sixteen chapters published
    on the site with a Lab Reports page for every report of the month.
    The plan's first step was built the same day: physical ranges for
    every distance, time and count of the brain; the wingman's five
    constants as weights; eight named personas; three escort gauntlets
    with a measure of station. The scripted wingman covers its leader
    (29 of 30) and comes back after a shell (30 of 30) and holds eight
    tiles for only 57 to 59% of a keep-station run, which is the first
    number a trained wingman has to beat. The one thing learned: a judge
    that reads a total cannot see what it does not weigh, and a search
    will spend itself on any number it is allowed to move.

INDEX TERMS

    post-mortem, ranges, decrees, wingman, personas, gauntlets, scale.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Paul set the day's mode: max fix. Read every lab report, write the
    long post-mortem, rewrite the goals and the how-to linking back,
    overview over specifics, prompts spell-corrected; then the plan:
    personas from the terrain's maxima, a wingman that follows the
    person, gauntlets, maybe a bigger transform, physical constraints
    locked into ranges first, a scale system, a zero-sum economy, the
    logistics of a base, play with bots with every feature an element,
    a testing plan, IEEE references, chaptered HTML on the site.

II. WHAT WAS DONE

    The morning before the mode began: the search had turned the base
    cycle off overnight at +0.83 on the score and lost the building fix
    with it; it was restored by hand and a guard added in orrery's
    planner (a candidate that gives up building, road or kills less
    deaths past a floor waits for a person); a pinned test was fixed.

    Then the reading, the book (orrery docs/book, built by chapters.py
    into reports/2026-11-02-the-plan), the Lab Reports page, and the
    code: RANGES, the wingman's weights, the personas, the gauntlets,
    rules::physical(). Tests: 59 and 151 pass. No default moved.

III. RESULTS

    Gauntlets, thirty seeds: G10 57 to 59% within eight tiles (0 of 30
    at a bar of seven tenths; the bar is a half); G11 29 of 30; G12 30
    of 30. Ranges: 57 physical, 24 shares, bio_period a decree at 60.
    Personas: 8, unread, their censuses asked for. References: 106, 36
    of them IEEE, each found and verified by search.

IV. CONCLUSION

    The second half of the design begins with constraints, not with
    more search. What is Paul's to decide is listed in the book's
    chapter 3 and in CI-084: the decree candidates, the reward for
    going forward, the persona names, and whether a person's ping is
    always a call.
