          A NIGHT ON THE MACHINE: THE SWITCHES, THE ISLAND SITE,
                              THE POSTURE

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           23 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The census machine came up (i7-13700F, 24 threads): LR 71 again
    in 977 s, the same to every digit. Sixteen networked clients
    played on one Hyprland screen. Each default switched off read the
    dodge, posture and priced_crossing as earning their place and
    island_site as costing 0.43 a seat, all of it on atlantic and
    europe. The base went across the sea; a 30-tile reach fixed it
    (+0.66 +/- 0.13, +4.95 on europe). SPSA tunes the posture overnight.

I. INTRODUCTION

    A PC with an RTX 4060 Ti joined the bench (copal, NVK). Paul asked
    for it to be worked hard.

II. BACKGROUND

    LR 22 to 25 the switches and the posture; LR 40 SPSA; LR 71 every
    map; LR 72 the machine.

III. METHOD

    The fair census from frozen binaries, split over 24 jobs, pooled
    by pool.sh; a meter line where a reading needed a cause.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. scripts/quad.sh on Hyprland: a grid of floating windows; the
       stagger 1 s -> 0.25 s, so all 16 find the lobby in its 8 s.
    2. The HUD's top and bottom strips at half height (9b293cb).
    3. LR 71 replayed: 977 s.
    4. switches.sh SPLIT (5eb9603); the switch census: eleven rows
       0.00, the switches already on.
    5. Each default off, 200 seeds: island_site +0.43, atlantic and
       europe.
    6. site_poor: no game changed. The meter: bases 70 to 275 tiles
       off. site_reach 30: +0.66 +/- 0.13, as island_site off on both
       maps.
    7. SPSA over seven posture weights, 64 steps; the unseen-seed
       census queued behind it.

V. RESULTS

    LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md section 73.

VI. DISCUSSION

    A switch list that outlives its promotions reads nothing. A
    per-map reading pointed at two maps; a meter line at the cause.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. board splits by map (island +3.98 off, naval -1.29 on).
    2. A third of a census's CPU is sys time.
    3. switches.sh's default list is the switches before promotion.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. The SPSA point against the defaults on 528 unseen seeds.
