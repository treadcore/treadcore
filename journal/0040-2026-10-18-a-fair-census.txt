            A FAIR CENSUS: EVERY TEAM IN THE TRIAL SEAT

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Journal 0038 found the brain census biased: on fixed seeds, the
    trial team drew the better starts (12.66 a seat against 6.44 with
    every team the same). The census now plays each seed three times,
    every team in the trial seat once. A null run reads exactly even,
    13.93 against 13.93. Measured again this way, the old castle scored
    8.36 a seat against the capped castle's 9.08 over nine naval
    matches. That agrees with the paired reading of 0038 (-0.96) and
    keeps the capped castle the default. `make tune` drops to two seeds
    a child, six matches.

I. METHOD

    `brain_census` (fleet.rs): `CENSUS_FAIR`, on by default, plays
    every (seed, trial team) pair; `CENSUS_FAIR=0` keeps one match a
    seed with the trial team rotated. A null run of one seed of 120 s
    first, then `CENSUS_CAPS=-castle_cap`: naval.map, three seeds of
    900 s, respawns 3.

II. RESULTS

    Null: 13.93 and 13.93. Old castle against capped: 8.36 and 9.08 a
    seat; 642 crossings searched and 3,356 answered from the cache;
    27.8 us a seat-tick.

III. DISCUSSION

    A census that can read a null run as even is one whose differences
    can be believed. The price is three times the matches.

IV. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Twelve seeds per capability, now that the census is fair.
