VI. DISCUSSION

    The first rule was the obvious one and it was wrong in a way only
    a bigger sample showed: at twenty seeds it moved a margin that
    was already one tank, and that could have been called noise. At
    sixty the shift was twenty tanks, and the cause was plain once
    looked for. The fuel rule is better because it is a fact about
    the tank, not about what one bot happened to see: a tank at full
    fuel has never driven, whoever looks at it.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

       photographed on a software rasteriser.

       A grace, not a shield; a playtest says whether it is enough.
    3. Carried: PLAYABILITY 6, 7, 9, 10, 11; the roster as a table;
