        BRIDGE JOBS: A PART-LAID BRIDGE WAITS ON THE TEAM BOARD

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    A bridge given up half laid used to stay in the water while its
    layer went on by boat. Under the new bit `bridge_jobs` (with
    `board`) each bridge being laid is posted to the team board with
    its tiles still to lay. Left part laid, it is taken by the next
    teammate with material within `bridge_reach` and laid from the
    laid end, and it comes off the board when laid or after
    `bridge_keep` untouched. In a test match a tank took a three-tile
    remainder and laid it by 9 s. The first census showed nobody
    taking anything: the takers allowed (tanks on auto, expanding or
    guarding) were never there, the wingmen were. A scalar,
    `bridge_wing`, lets them. The fair census then read 7.31 a seat
    against 8.01, inside the noise; the bit stays off.

I. METHOD

    Board: `BridgeJob`, `post_bridge`, `prune_bridges`, `take_bridge`.
    Hop: `bridge_job`, `adopt_bridge`, `bridge_refused`; an adopted
    bridge's trip ends when it is laid or given up. Commander:
    `bridge_jobs`, called where the board is posted. Weights
    `bridge_keep` 180, `bridge_reach` 40, `bridge_wing` 1 (rounded).
    Census: `CENSUS_BASE_CAPS=board CENSUS_CAPS=bridge_jobs`,
    naval.map, two seeds of 900 s, respawns 3; and a "for the team"
    count.

II. RESULTS

    Brain tests 60 passed, 8 ignored; client 31. One match with the
    first rule: 0 seat-seconds for the team. A temporary line every 10
    s (removed) showed free bridges of 2 to 17 tiles near wingmen with
    8 material and the leader, who had given them up, the only tank
    on auto. With `bridge_wing`: 117 seat-seconds in that match. Fair
    census: 7.31 against 8.01; acting in five of six matches, 69 to
    783 seat-seconds; one trial team laid 36 bridge tiles.

III. DISCUSSION

    The plan put the job on the board for "the next tank with
    material", and the code first read that as the next idle tank. On
    naval.map the leader is the only tank on auto in the field, so the
    bridge waited for nobody. Counting before guessing found it in one
    match. Whether bridges pay is not settled by six matches, and
    probably not in 900 s: a finished bridge is worth what the road
    network later carries over it.

IV. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Longer matches for `escort` and `bridge_jobs`, both of which pay
       late (goal 5).
    2. A dodge for direct fire (12d).
    3. The `why` and `doing` strings as codes, and the Observation
       shared per team (12b).
