                        THE LANDFALL AS A SCENARIO

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           20 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Scenarios can now carve a sea, put a tank afloat, set teams and
    weights, recall drones, and check where a boat came ashore. The
    landfall's memory has a scenario that holds at the default and
    fails with the memory off. Three versions before it tested
    nothing; each found something: a Commander at sea skips the
    landfall (backlog 43), the first choice binds, and a drone sees
    for its tank.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. teams, terrain, tank, centre, weights, lands_near.
    2. A Commander at sea: `fortify` throughout; memory on and off
       alike. The afloat branch never ran.
    3. A RuleBot, weights as fields: west both times. The choice made
       at t = 0, with the gun seen.
    4. Seen ashore, then put to sea: west both times. The status text
       added to the trace; refusals recorded (none).
    5. The drone: two recalled at the move; memory on west, off east.

VI. DISCUSSION

    A scenario that cannot fail tests nothing. Running it broken first
    is what found the gap, the binding choice and the drone.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Backlog 43, the Commander at sea, as a scenario first.
