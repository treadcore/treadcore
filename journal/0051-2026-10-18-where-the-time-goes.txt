          WHICH RUNGS THE LEAN MOVES, AND WHERE THE TIME GOES

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    New instruments, with no change to play: the rung each think took,
    each changed pick by rung, and every seat-second by status code.
    The posture's lean moves tanks from Fortify to Expand, and turret
    time falls 88%. Economy feeds back on building, but turning it off
    restores half the turret time and none of the score (+0.13 +/-
    0.47), so the hypothesis is not confirmed. The larger finding: the
    Auto ladder decides 5.6% of a team's seat-seconds, the island hop
    19%, and 46.7% is spent dead. The census measures attrition.

I. INTRODUCTION

    Backlog 27, set by journal 0050: which rungs the lean moves, and
    whether Economy's lean costs the point.

II. BACKGROUND

    Leaning the ladder reads about a point behind in both forms of the
    posture (0050). The environment as before.

III. METHOD

    `Brain::rung`, `Meter::moved`, `CENSUS_ACTS`. Three 48-seed runs on
    the same seeds: no lean, lean, lean with `w_pos_economy` 0. One
    16-seed null run for the time budget.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. The instruments. A 300 s trial match: the trial team's five
       tanks on the ladder 22 seat-seconds of 1,500.
    2. The first launch passed its label to env as a command and ran
       nothing; a script fixed it.
    3. Lean and null at 48 seeds. The lean's per-seed differences were
       those of 0050 to the hundredth. Fortify to Expand 84% of changed
       picks; Turrets 8,427 to 1,026 seat-seconds. The summary line
       printed no moves: the total did not add the new matrix; fixed,
       and this run's per-match lines summed.
    4. Economy off: 44,975 changed picks, Turrets 3,961, -0.92 +/- 0.54;
       against Economy on, +0.13 +/- 0.47. Not confirmed.
    5. The ladder held about 5% of the trial seat-seconds, so every one
       was counted by code: dead 46.7%, hop 18.9%, rotation 7.3%,
       ladder 5.6%, dodge 4.5%.
    6. `make test`: green (results in V).

V. RESULTS

    See CI-003 section IV and LR 26. Deaths: 3.4 a seat a match against
    3 respawns. Tests: brain 68 passed and 8 ignored; client 31; net 14;
    proto 3; sim 50.

VI. DISCUSSION

    Three steps of work went into a part that holds 5.6% of the time.
    The time budget should come first: it is now one switch. Half the
    team dead means every census so far has measured who dies first,
    and the parts that pay late never got to.
