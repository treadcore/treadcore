                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org

ABSTRACT

    Backlog 48, the starting numbers. `make parts` turns each of 31
    parts off from the brain with every part on, at 12 seeds. One
    part reads past two errors, the dodge, and it pays (+5.20 +/-
    1.91). The two parts the hypothesis blamed read as paying. Their
    weights halved read +0.61 +/- 0.60 at 48 seeds; the lowest row,
    `landfall_wait`, halved, -0.09 +/- 0.46. The defaults stand.

INDEX TERMS

    tuning, ablation, census guard.

I. INTRODUCTION

    "continue." The hypothesis: one or two parts (the posture's lean,
    `team_rotate`) carry LR 55's -0.85 for every part on, and halving
    their weight gives it back.

II. BACKGROUND

    `make switches` measures each part that is off, turned on. With
    every part on since LR 55 there was no reverse. 101 weights, 17
    bits; seven families with floors.

III. METHOD

    Each part off at 12 seeds from a frozen binary, four censuses at
    once; then the candidates at 48 seeds, pooled over their per-seed
    differences.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `scripts/parts.sh`, `make parts`: 17 capabilities as `-name`,
       14 weights at their before-values. A smoke run of two parts at
       one seed of 30 s.
    2. The full run, 31 censuses, about 1.5 h. The dodge alone past
       two errors, paying. The posture +1.82, `team_rotate` +0.83.
       The lowest: `landfall_wait` -2.03, `kite` -1.76, `duck` -1.57.
    3. The plan's proof anyway, `w_posture` 0.25 and `swap_gap` 0.6
       at 48 seeds: +0.61 +/- 0.60. With it, the lowest row halved:
       -0.09 +/- 0.46.
    4. The defaults kept. The bottom of the table to backlog 53.

V. RESULTS

    LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md section 63. Tests: brain 83 passed, 11
    ignored; client 32; net 14; proto 3; sim 50. Seven families above
    their floors. No brain code changed.

VI. DISCUSSION

    LR 55's cost is not a part. Twelve seeds rank; they do not
    decide. The table says where to spend the 48-seed censuses.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS
