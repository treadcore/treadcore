           THE CAPPED CASTLE BY DEFAULT, AND A BIASED CENSUS

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The castle of roadmap 12h (two pillboxes inside the wall, one
    outside beside its road, then posts along the roads to the side's
    other bases) is now every Commander's castle; the castle of 11b and
    11h is one capability bit away. Its two proofs were kept with the
    bit off; brain tests 55 passed. Measuring the change found a flaw
    in the census: with every team on the same settings, the team in
    the trial seat scored 12.66 a seat against 6.44, because the fixed
    seeds deal it the better starts. Read paired against that null
    run, the old castle scored 0.96 a seat less than the capped one:
    no measurable cost.

INDEX TERMS

    capability flags, paired comparison, null run, fortification.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Journal 0037 built the capped castle behind `castle_cap`, off by
    default under the rule that a census promotes a part. Paul chose to
    make it the default. This entry is that transition and what the
    measurement of it showed.

II. METHOD

    1. `CASTLE_CAP` on in `Caps::BASELINE` (weights.rs).
    2. The two proofs of the old castle, `a_commander_fortifies_its_depot`
       (three towers) and `a_commander_climbs_the_ladder` (two bastions),
       given the old castle explicitly (`bastions()`: the default less
       the bit).
    3. The census learned `-name` in `CENSUS_CAPS`. Two runs on
       naval.map, six seeds of 900 s, respawns 3: the old castle as the
       trial, and the default against itself (the null run).

III. RESULTS

        run                     trial team   baseline teams
        null (default x 3)         12.66          6.44
        old castle, trial          11.70          7.67

    Trial a seat by seed, null: 6.39, 13.35, 12.51, 21.01, 11.54,
    11.15; old castle: 6.96, 11.84, 10.48, 14.87, 21.58, 4.45.

IV. DISCUSSION

    The null run should have read even and read six points apart: the
    trial team's starts are better on these seeds. Every earlier census
    figure of the form "trial against baseline" carries that bias; the
    tuner's fitness does too, but evenly across its children, so its
    ranking holds. The rule in ROADMAP goal 4 now reads the census
    paired, and backlog item 16 asks for the cure: every team in the
    trial seat on every seed.

V. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Deal the trial team every start on every seed (backlog 16).
    2. The first seed's castles finish after 900 s under either rule:
       longer matches (goal 5) will show the cap's effect better.
