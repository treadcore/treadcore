## Abstract

The plan's last two stages of the economy, the same night as the second.
Game 7: wells of oil for the refineries and the depots' fuel; the workers
as stock in transit, a smelter's shells and a depot's shells for its
pillboxes on figures that walk the roads, lost to a splash, taken by a
tank; supply earned from what a side holds and destroys and spent as one
side on the depot's stores; the guard as quartermaster, building the
town, the works and the city when quiet. Game 8: convoys of supply
between a side's depots by road or by sea, both ends paid by the
distance; a raider that takes them and a cover that drives at the
raider. Five logistics gauntlets from the deposit through the factory
and the port to the city, with the reward terms a course can name, and
seventeen senses so a net can learn them. The scripted brain passes all
five at ten of ten; the nets pass none that needs the squad. The lab
report of the economy is on the site.

## Session

Stage 3 first: the sim's wells, workers, supply and the stores, with the
tests; then the brain's city and the oil run; the first end-to-end run
showed no oil pumped because the zone's refineries were neutral and
wanted their plant, and that the guard could not buy with its own
supply, so a side spends as one and the city takes the plant first.

Stage 4: trade. The first cut paid every pair of a side's depots both
ways every minute: 16,951 supply for one team in five minutes. One
convoy a depot a round, to its nearest partner, at half the rate.

Then Paul's gauntlets. G13, G14, G16 and G17 held on the first write.
G15 took four: a rule bot by the road took the far depot (a side of one
depot trades with nobody); a sitter at full armour outlived two convoys;
a sitter ten tiles out of sight was never covered; the sitter's death
ended a two-player match before the first convoy left. It reads every
brain alike now, and wants a depot a scenario can hold against capture.

The senses grew by seventeen, which broke the shipped transform (made
for 153 inputs) and the widening of old nets (a constant written as
"the senses less the last two blocks"); both read again.
