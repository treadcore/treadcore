                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org

ABSTRACT

    Backlog 33. A boat whose chosen beach a seen hostile tank covers
    now stands off out of its reach for up to `landfall_wait` seconds
    of the crossing (0 by Paul's choice), with a status code,
    `stand_off`. At 10 over 48 seeds: -0.51 a seat, error 0.63; the
    landfall deaths did not fall, and the stand-off fired under 0.07%
    of the time. The guns that sink boats are rarely at the beach.
    Three landfall parts have each assumed where the gun is; backlog
    34 reads it.

III. METHOD

    LR 33: the part in `landfall_order`, its test, the census at
    `landfall_wait = 10` with `CENSUS_ACTS`.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. Paul set the default: "yes 0".
    2. The part, the code, the test; the weight placed after
       `landfall_shy`.
    3. The census: -0.85, -0.53, -0.15 by range; -0.51 +/- 0.63.
       Landfall deaths 573 of 2,460; stand-off below the 40 commonest
       codes.
    4. Paul: "commit. push. continue backlog" while the census ran;
       the code waited for its measurement, three minutes.
    5. `make brainmap` showed `standing_off` taking `landfall_order`'s
       doc comment: placed between them. Moved.

VI. DISCUSSION

    A part that never fires cannot be measured by its score. The
    status code made that visible in one table. Each new part should
    be read for how often it acts before its score is read.
