     THE UPGRADES PROPOSAL: SUPPLY, SIXTEEN UPGRADES, THE WORKS TAB

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Roadmap 11g waited since 15 October on one question, in-match or
    carried between matches. It is answered in-match and written up
    as a proposal, documentation/PROPOSAL-UPGRADES.md, in the form
    GOVERNANCE.md section 5 asks for. A side earns supply from what it
    holds (6 a minute a pillbox, 12 a depot) and from kills and
    captures, and spends it on twelve upgrades for a base or the side
    and four for the tank. It buys them at a depot from a works tab on
    the coordination screen; the squad installs those that need
    building. Bots buy through the base cycle's improvement slot. The
    proposal changes the rules and the wire (version 17), so nothing
    of it is built yet. It waits on its seven days of discussion and
    on the two traces its section 6 names.

I. INTRODUCTION

    Paul asked for "more base upgrades and ways for users to purchase
    them", then for the proposal itself. The brain already has a place
    to spend: the improvement slot and the weight `upgrade_vs_mend`
    (journal 0037). The game had nothing to sell.

II. METHOD

    The numbers were taken from the rules as they stand (PILLBOX_RANGE
    8, PILLBOX_HEALTH 60, TANK_ARMOR 100, PILLBOY_MAX 4,
    PILLBOY_MATERIAL_MAX 8, BOAT_COST 5, the base alarm at 16 tiles).
    The earning rate was set from the census of journal 0038, where a
    team held 2 to 6 pillboxes and depots on average, so that the first
    upgrade (60 supply) comes in reach between the second and the fifth
    minute. Every price is marked as a first guess for the traces.

III. RESULTS

    The proposal: supply's sources and rates; sixteen upgrades with
    levels, effects, supply and material prices and how each is
    installed; `Command::Buy`, `PillboyTask::Install`, `Event::Bought`,
    `Event::Installed`; the change crate by crate; what must not change
    (determinism, old replays under `Scenario::upgrades` false, a match
    without purchases unchanged, nothing carried between matches); nine
    named proofs and a playtest; four questions for the discussion.
    The roadmap (11g, backlog 14), the index, the lab report (section
    13) and the changelog carry it.

IV. DISCUSSION

    Supply is the ledger's `held_s` made a currency. The census already
    measures what the economy would pay. Holding ground becomes what
    the bots are scored on (`long_game`) and what buys them their next
    advantage, which is how a long match is meant to work.

V. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. Post the proposal for its seven days (Discussions, under
       Proposals). That is outward-facing, and Paul's to do or to ask
       for.
    2. The two traces of its section 6, which need the earning in the
       sim with buying switched off: a small first sim step that
       changes no rule.
    3. The gatehouse needs a tile the side's tanks can cross. It may
       want its own step first.
