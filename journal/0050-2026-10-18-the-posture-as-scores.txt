                        THE POSTURE AS SCORES

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           18 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The board's posture was a first-match ladder on thresholds; LR 9
    designed five scores. Each is now a score from 0 to 1 with its own
    weight, blended with the old ladder by `posture_scored`, which is 1
    by default; 0 gives the ladder's pick. A test shows each posture
    winning on its own case, three of which the ladder called Fortify.
    Over the same 48 seeds as journal 0049, the scored posture under
    `posture` read -1.04 +/- 0.58 a seat, the ladder's -0.81 +/- 0.65:
    -0.23 +/- 0.74 apart. The two together read -0.93 +/- 0.49, so the
    lean, not the posture, is the suspect. `posture` stays off.

INDEX TERMS

    posture, utility scores, influence map, paired comparison.


I. INTRODUCTION

    Backlog 21, the next step journal 0049 set: finding A3 of CI-001,
    the posture scored as designed.

II. BACKGROUND

    `posture` measured at -0.81 +/- 0.65 with the ladder's posture
    (0049). The environment as before.

III. METHOD

    Scores from LR 9's table; the plan's progress passed to the board
    by the member rebuilding it (`Home`); the same 48 seeds as 0049, in
    three ranges of 16, compared seed by seed with 0049's run.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `Plan::castle_left`, `Plan::slots_left`; `Member.material`;
       `Home`; `PostureScores`; the blend; six weights.
    2. First draft: with no hostile in sight the odds were press_odds,
       so Expand scored 1 at every quiet start. LR 9 says "front odds":
       none seen is even, Expand 0.
    3. `each_posture_scores_its_own_case` passed on the first run; each
       case's scores printed and read: each wins for its own reason.
    4. The census: +0.40, -1.46 and -2.07 by range (the last alone
       outside two errors); -1.04 +/- 0.58 over 48. Seed by seed
       against 0049: -0.23 +/- 0.74. Together: -0.93 +/- 0.49.
    5. `make test`: green.

V. RESULTS

    Postures read (economy / fortify / connect / expand / defend): the
    ladder's 16 / 47 / 19 / 18 / 0.4%, scored 37 / 43 / 6 / 15 / 0.5%.
    Tests: brain 68 passed and 8 ignored; client 31; net 14; proto 3;
    sim 50. Weights: 83.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The posture changed what the board reads, but not the score. What
    both forms share is the lean over the ladder's own order, and that
    reads about a point behind. The ladder's order was tuned by play.
    A lean that lifts Expand over Fortify whenever material runs low
    may simply be worse play, and the next step counts which rungs it
    moves.

    3. The board's inputs come from whoever rebuilds it (CI-001 A5).
