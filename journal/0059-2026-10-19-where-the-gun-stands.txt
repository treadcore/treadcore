                  WHERE THE GUN THAT SINKS THE BOAT STANDS

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           19 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    Backlog 34's read. At each landfall death the census now knows the
    boat's chosen beach (`Brain::beach`) and measures the killer's
    place. Over 164 deaths: the killer stood a median 8 tiles from the
    boat, inside its 10-tile range and at the edge of sight; 38% stood
    within reach of the beach, 60% nearer the boat than the beach, 9%
    afloat. A boat sees its killer only once it can fire. That
    explains LR 31 to 33's three nulls, and corrects LR 32's "not
    blind". Backlog 35: price the landfall by the team board's
    hostile influence, which nothing reads.

III. METHOD

    LR 34: `Brain::beach`; at each landfall death with a killer, four
    numbers; quartiles by range. Null run, 16 seeds in three ranges.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `Brain::beach` (default None); RuleBot's `landfall`, the
       Commander's inner's.
    2. The read: killer to boat 8, to beach 29, boat to beach 20, by
       median; at the beach 38%, nearer the boat 60%, afloat 9%.
    3. LR 32 marked; backlog 34 closed; 35 written.

VI. DISCUSSION

    LR 32 counted a sighting in the last 10 s as "seen" without asking
    from how far. The distance was the question. A sighting is useful
    only beyond the gun's range, and with sight equal to range there is
    none. The team board's influence is the one store of knowledge
    beyond sight the bots already build.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. Paul: the default for `landfall_board` (backlog 35).
    2. Backlog 35's read: the board's hostile influence where the
       killers stood.

APPENDIX B. ARTIFACTS
