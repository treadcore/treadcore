ABSTRACT

    Version 0.1.0-rc.1 is offered as the first release candidate
    under RELEASE.md section 4. To get there the session wrote the
    release procedure as a script, `make release`, whose point is the
    pictures: it refuses a release whose documents do not show the
    window it ships, which is the mistake journal 0017 found (three
    empty frames in the gallery). `make shoot` drives the window
    through one scripted match and crops twelve frames; the player's
    guide, HOW-TO-PLAY.md, is written against them; the check holds
    the two together. The crates were published to crates.io in
    dependency order. The candidate is confirmed by the steward's own
    playtest on one machine, as section 4 allows until there are
    three playtesters, and the notes say so.

INDEX TERMS

    Then the guide written against the frames. Then the check, which

VI. DISCUSSION

    The release procedure is a check on documents, not on code: the
    code has `make test` and the replay. What a release can get
    wrong that a test cannot catch is showing a window that is not
    the one it ships, and the only way to make that impossible is to
    have the pictures taken by a script from the build, and to have
    a script refuse a release whose documents do not show them. The
    manifest is the hinge: it names the frames this version took,
    and the check reads the documents for each name.

    The guide was written from the frames, not the other way round,
    and that is the right order: a sentence about a lamp strip that
    sits under a picture of the lamp strip cannot drift from it
    without the picture being retaken, and the check makes the
    retake happen.
