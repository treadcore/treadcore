               THE METHOD, A MACHINE FOR IT, AND COMPETITIONS

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           22 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    LR 71 final: +5.94 +/- 0.38 over 176 seeds. The census costs
    cores, not memory: 3.4 CPU-hours, 5 MB a job, a third of a core
    each in the VM. A manual, RESUME, and CLAUDE.md ready it for a
    dedicated machine. LR 72 draws the method and opens competitions.

I. INTRODUCTION

    Paul offered a server or a new PC and asked what would speed the
    census. Measured first: the VM starved it of CPU.

II. BACKGROUND

    LR 14 the fair census, LR 24 the noise, LR 41 the frozen binary,
    LR 71 every map.

III. METHOD

    ps and free during the run; pool.sh at its end; a 2-seed census
    stopped and resumed, its logs compared.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. The census ended overnight: pooled.txt, 176 seeds.
    2. RAM 5 MB a job; CPU a third of a core a job.
    3. "DDR2, 48 threads" does not fit two sockets: asked for lscpu.
    4. LR 71 final; the meter recounted (the end summaries had
       doubled it). Pushed.
    5. RESUME and run.env in census.sh; JOBS from nproc; checked
       deterministic on resume.
    6. CENSUS.md, CLAUDE.md, LR 72 with the loop drawn, backlog 58
       to 61.

V. RESULTS

    LAB-REPORT-BRAIN.md sections 71 and 72; CI-041.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The method is directed changes tested by paired Monte Carlo.
    With a machine, the question can move from "is this part worth
    anything" to "which style beats which".

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. The server's real core count, and LR 71 timed on it.
    2. Read sooner (59); profiles (60); rotation (61).

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. The machine set up by CENSUS.md section 5.

APPENDIX B. ARTIFACTS

    section 72; CI-041.
