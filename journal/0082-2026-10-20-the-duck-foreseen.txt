ABSTRACT

    A family of a tank under a hostile gun about to be ready put the
    duck at 76.6% (63.7% without it). Traced: the duck re-picked its
    point every think and pivoted in place; and a dodging tank whose
    own gun came ready fired back, which turns the whole hull, and ran
    into the splash. `duck_plan` holds its point and foresees one out of
    reach by the gun's ready tick: 83.4%. `dodge_fire` holds a shot that
    would ruin a dodge. The guard: +0.21 +/- 0.68; own-shell catches
    ashore 6.1% to 1.6%.

IV. CHRONOLOGY

    1. `[[tank]] around`, `reload`; the family; `SET` refused by a
       RuleBot, so it takes only the landfall's weights.
    2. 67.0% without the duck, 86.2% with it, the gun at a fixed 0.9 s.
    3. Traces: the jitter; a hit at 2.85 s after a turn of 123 degrees
       in one tick. Not a steering bug: the tank fired. The sim turns
       the hull to every shot.
    4. `dodge_fire`: 86.5%. Hits classed by damage: 13 of 21 a direct
       shot at 1.3 s, mostly beyond any duck's reach in 0.9 s.
    5. The gun's ready time drawn from 0.4 to 1.4 s, as the file had
       said and not done. `duck_plan`: 76.6% to 83.4%.
    6. The `dodge_fire` test on seed 4 held broken once `duck_plan`
       changed the duck; seed 261 found by search, 2 of 400 seeds.
    7. The guard census: +0.21 +/- 0.68.

VI. DISCUSSION

    go, when an errand may go back, and whether a shot is worth its
    turn. Each was a case the bot could not see because it chose by
    geometry and not by motion.
