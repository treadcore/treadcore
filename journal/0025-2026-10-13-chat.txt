                 CHAT: A LINE ON THE NEWSWIRE

                     Paul Richeson, treadcore.org
                           13 October 2026


ABSTRACT

    The roadmap's backlog is closed; PLAYABILITY.md had seven items
    open, and chat was first: sixteen players and alliances with no
    words. `Command::Chat` and its team routing were in the sim since
    step 6 with no way to type. Now Enter opens a line on the newswire
    to everyone, Shift-Enter to the team, Tab switches, Enter sends, Esc
    puts it away. The sim enforces SPEC UI-23's one message a second,
    and cuts a message to 120 characters with no control characters.
    Driving the window with xdotool found one bug that tests could not:
    keys arriving in the same frame as the Enter that opened the line
    were thrown away.

INDEX TERMS

    chat, text input, flood limit.


I. INTRODUCTION

    "Nothing to say" (PLAYABILITY item 6): the window had no box for
    the command the world already carried.

II. BACKGROUND

    Journal 0024 ended with sound. Open in PLAYABILITY: 6 chat,
    7 proposals, 9 rookie bots, 10 the second match, 11 a minimap,
    12 smoothing (leave until seen), 13 a measurement. The sim's chat
    had no limit on length or rate: a 10 KB message would have been a
    10 KB event to every client.

III. METHOD

    1. The world enforces, the window helps. The sim cuts and strips
       and refuses a second message inside CHAT_EVERY_TICKS (one
       second, UI-23's floor) with `Player::last_chat`, which is part
       of the hashed world; no replay fixture is committed, so no
       recording breaks. The window holds a line it may not send yet
       ("say to all (a second)>") instead of letting the world refuse
       it and lose the words.
    2. Enter was the tank view's second key. SPEC UI-13 asks for a
       "tank view key"; F is it. Enter went to chat, as the plan in
       PLAYABILITY said.
    3. The keys are the line's while it is open. input.rs reads no key
       while typing (the mouse and the order tiles still work); the
       chat system runs after input.rs's, so the Esc that closes the
       line has already been ignored by the Esc that quits. The sound's
       M is a letter while typing.
    4. A message is held on the newswire for eight seconds over later
       events, by the sender's name, with the notice tone at a third.
    5. Not done: UI-22's "all nearby tanks" and "players selected".

IV. CHRONOLOGY

     1. Sim: `chat`, the limits, CHAT_EVERY_TICKS and CHAT_MAX_CHARS in
        rules.rs; the alliance test extended (refused inside a second,
        cut to 120, control characters gone, empty refused).
     2. Window: chat.rs with a pure `edit` (three tests), the View's
        typing, chat_sent and chat; the newswire's order (the line,
        a refusal, a message, the last event); input.rs's keys gated;
        F alone for the tank view; the key hint's fourth line.
     3. Driven with xdotool under X11, as the shoot does. The first run
        "failed" on my script, not the window: step 2 took longer than
        a second, and an extra Enter opened an empty line that the
        Shift-Enter then closed, so "mmm flank" went to the game as
        three M presses and Esc quit. Retimed.
     4. The second run: "ello from the window". xdotool sends Enter and
        the first letters in one frame; the system opened the line and
        cleared the frame's other keys. Rewritten to take the frame's
        keys in order. Third run: the message whole; the second message
        held inside the second and sent on the next Enter; a team line
        with "mmm flank" in it (no sound toggled), put away by Esc with
        the window still running; Tab to the team, sent.
     5. Frames of the line being typed, of the held line, and of a
        message on the wire; the hint's fourth line fits.

V. RESULTS

    make test passes (client 12, sim 24 with the chat checks); no
    warnings. The drive: 3 messages sent as typed, 1 held, 0 lost
    letters, 0 orders or sound toggles from typed keys, Esc closed the
    line and did not quit.

VI. DISCUSSION

    The lost first letter is the session's lesson: a pure function was
    tested and correct, and the system around it dropped input at a
    frame boundary that only a fast typist, or a script, crosses.
    Driving the real window found it in one run.

VII. OPEN PROBLEMS

    1. UI-22's nearby and selected recipients.
    2. The key hint changed, so the release frames differ from those
       in reports/images until the next shoot.
    3. Bots neither read nor write chat.

VIII. NEXT STEPS

    1. PLAYABILITY item 7: proposals in the window (Y/N to answer, a P
       menu to make one). Chat is half of what an alliance needs; this
       is the other half.
